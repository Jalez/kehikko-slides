- **Errors are reported.** If Slides' page fails to draw, Kehikot now says why instead of showing a blank container.
- **No flash while it opens.** Kehikot's own cover stays up until the deck has drawn, so you no longer see it half-opened.
- **Opens in the pane you left.** Edit or Preview comes back when you return to the kehikko.
- **Decks is a switch.** The Decks menu now marks the deck that is open instead of being a plain list to jump from, and the header no longer carries "Slide x of y": where you are in the deck is in the footer.
