Kind: feature

- **An audience view for your talk.** In the presenter view, "Audience view" shows a link with Copy and Open in browser. Paste it into any browser on this computer and that window shows only the slide, as large as it fits on black, and moves the moment you do. Press F there for full screen.
- **Nothing for the audience to touch.** That window has no notes, no timer and no buttons, and it cannot change anything. Several windows can follow one talk, and the presenter view says how many do.
- **Private by default.** The link carries a random key that works only on this computer, and "New link" stops the old one at once. If you close the presenter view, the audience window keeps the last slide and quietly says "Presenter disconnected", then carries on when you come back; after half an hour away the link stops working.
