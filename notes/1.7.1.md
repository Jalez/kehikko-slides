- **Slides opens where you left it.** The deck you had open, whether you were editing or previewing, and whether the slides follow the paper stay as you set them on each kehikko, also after Kehikot restarts.
- **Decks is a switch.** The Decks menu now marks the deck that is open instead of being a plain list to jump from, and the footer, not the header, says where you are in the deck.
- **Agents can propose a change to a deck instead of writing it.** A proposal is drawn over the deck as a diff with Accept, Decline and Edit, then accept; nothing is written until you accept, and Undo takes an accepted one back. The footer counts the proposals waiting.
- **Errors are reported, and no flash while it opens.** If Slides' page fails to draw, Kehikot says why, and its cover stays up until the deck has drawn.
