- **Agents can propose a change to a deck instead of writing it.** A proposal is drawn over the deck as a diff — what is there in red, what is proposed in green, the agent's reason beside it — with Accept, Decline, Decline with reason and Edit, then accept; a checkbox per change takes part of it. Nothing is written until you accept, and an accepted proposal goes into the deck's history like any other agent write, so Undo takes it back.
- **The footer counts the proposals waiting**, with Previous and Next proposal beside the count, and says why a proposal can no longer be applied when the deck changed under it.

For agents: new tools `propose_change`, `list_proposals`, `read_proposal` and `withdraw_proposal`. Prefer `propose_change` over `edit_slide` for a change the person should judge; `list_proposals` shows what became of each proposal, with the reason when it was declined — read it before proposing on that deck again.
